Fastest: install from your browser

10 seconds, no security warnings

  1. Open LAAS HQ in Chrome or Edge and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (or menu → Cast, save and share → Install page as app).
  2. In Safari on Mac: File → Add to Dock.
  3. LAAS HQ now has its own icon and window, and updates by itself.

Install on Mac

About a minute

  1. Open LAAS-HQ.dmg and drag LAAS HQ into Applications.
  2. Double-click LAAS HQ. The first time, your Mac says it can't check the app: click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to LAAS HQ. From then on it opens like any other app.
  4. Sign in with your LAAS HQ email and password. You stay signed in.

Why the extra step: the app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks once. It's our own app and only opens LAAS HQ. After the first time it opens like any other app.

Install on Windows

About a minute

  1. Open LAAS-HQ-Setup.exe. It installs for your user only, no admin needed.
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC": click More info → Run anyway.
  3. LAAS HQ opens by itself and is in the Start menu and on your desktop.
  4. Sign in with your LAAS HQ email and password. You stay signed in.

Why the warning: SmartScreen warns about apps that aren't code-signed yet. It's our own app and only opens LAAS HQ.
